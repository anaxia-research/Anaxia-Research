- [ ] Compile and visually inspect the final submission PDF.
- [ ] Review the current [Preprints.org author instructions](https://www.preprints.org/instructions-for-authors), posting/withdrawal policies, and any target journal's preprint policy.

No closure paper for Hodge, Yang–Mills, the Riemann hypothesis, or a reflexive-polytope descent bound should be inferred from finite computations, a known `D4` construction, or elementary modular examples. Such a paper requires a genuine complete result and independent mathematical review.
